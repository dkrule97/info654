2025

Greg Freeman - Burnover

I am completely obsessed with this album and this man. I had heard a couple of songs from his 2022 album “I Looked Out” but I didn’t really get into the canon until this year with the release of “Burnover.” On a whim, I bought cheap last-minute tickets to see him open for Grandaddy. He is incredible live. The show sold me on him. I was captivated.

“Burnover” is both ambitious and familiar. In the comments of the music video for “Curtain” someone said something along the lines of “song so good I need a thesaurus.” I think that sums Greg Freeman up pretty well. Soft spoken and smart, his songs are imbued with a sincerity I don’t often see. Coupled with his stellar guitar and the full five-piece band (saxophone included), the richness of Freeman’s songwriting is at the forefront.

Favorite Track: Salesman

Alex G - Headlights

A major label debut that keeps its weirdness, Alex G’s “Headlights” is as good as he’s ever been. He doesn’t really have a bad album, but the bigger production budget on this one lends some extra finesse. He just has such a way with layering sounds, I’m always impressed.

Favorite Track: Far and Wide

Friendship - Caveman Wakes Up

This was my first introduction to Friendship and I’m addicted. They find a delicate sadness and intensity in the mundane. It’s good crying music, which is my favorite kind. They look at city life in the same way my depressed brain does. Sometimes it’s so beautiful and human and other times it’s the most alienating place you can be. The vocals are wavering and strained, which is also very my taste.

Favorite Track: Free Association

Truman Sinclair - American Recordings

I’m a sucker for a solid debut, and this is a really strong one. It’s pretty much exactly what you expect it to be from the title and the cover art, but it’s executed so well that it doesn’t feel tired. There are so many great tracks from the memorable opener, “Chicago Flood,” to the sweeping storytelling of “Joel Roberts.”

Favorite Track: What's On Your Mind

Wednesday - Bleeds

Wednesday does it again. Karly is just so cool. I saw them at Brooklyn Steel about a month ago. She was playing her button-adorned guitar and wearing a piano-inspired tube top. Incredibly hot.

Every album of theirs builds on the previous one while deviating with just enough new stuff to keep me hooked. Each one is its own world, and “Bleeds” is no exception in its exceptionalness.

Favorite Track: Bitter Everyday

Water From Your Eyes - It's a Beautiful Place

There are a few filler tracks, but the singles rule. I’m not usually a “shredding on guitar” type of girl, but it’s done so artfully that I don’t mind. This album feels like an intimate look into a friendship, especially the fun parts. I came from the “This is Lorelei” camp, but I stayed for Rachel Brown.

Favorite Track: Nights in Armor

Hut Hut

Adrianne Lenker - Live at Revolution Hall

Lenker at her best. With an expansive tracklist that includes unreleased gems, “Live at Revolution Hall” is connective tissue between her and her fans. It’s everything we’ve wanted from her. She’s just herself (as best as I can tell), and I love it.

Favorite Track: not a lot, just forever
